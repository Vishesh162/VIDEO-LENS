Field notes: the art of noticing

Video notes
Overview Ready to read

Your video summary.

Good field notes begin with observation. The lesson uses flowers as a starting point: look for changes in light, movement, and texture before deciding what the scene means. Separate what you can see from what you assume, and keep a record you can return to.

The practice is repeatable: choose one detail, describe it precisely, then compare it with a second observation. Small, specific notes make a stronger record than a general impression.

A few details about the recordings, the notes, and what happens next.

What video can I upload?

Bring an MP4, MOV, WebM, or MKV video, or an MP3, WAV, M4A, or OGG recording. Files can be up to 200 MB.

Can I use a YouTube link?

Yes. Paste a link to a public YouTube video. The assistant works with the audio to prepare your transcript and notes.

Does it understand Hinglish?

Choose English or Hinglish before you start. Hinglish recordings are translated into English for the transcript and notes.

Where do video answers come from?

The assistant uses the video’s transcript. If the recording doesn’t contain the information, it tells you instead of filling in the gaps.

Can I keep my video notes?

Your library stays in this browser. Download notes as Markdown or copy the transcript to keep a separate record.
